# **Þræscype**

## *A fractured world. One forgotten beginning.*

When the world broke, it didn’t shatter — it multiplied.  
Twenty fragments now drift through the void, stitched together by portals and memory. You awaken in one of them, nameless and unmoored, surrounded by wanderers who remember too much or too little.

Each voice you meet carries a piece of the story:  

- **Eadric the Withered**, who clings to heirlooms of a vanished life.
- **Raevanna**, the witch whispered to be older than empires.  
- **Osric**, **Eanflaed**, **Cenric**, and others who speak of clouds, fractures, and things that still dream beneath the ruins.

Through dialogue and discovery, you trace the echoes of a catastrophe — a weapon, a rebellion, a love letter carved into stone. The truth hides in relics and stories and quests, in the quiet between worlds.

But *Þræscype* is not a story told to you.  
It’s one you assemble — piece by piece, choice by choice — until the fragments begin to remember themselves.

---

### **Features**

- **Dynamic dialogue trees** that evolve with your actions and inventory.  
- **Quest‑driven storytelling** woven through character encounters and environmental clues.  
- **A world of memory and consequence**, where every item returned and every word spoken reshapes what remains.  
- **Minimalist atmosphere** — monochrome, ancient, and dreamlike.

---

### **Tone**

Bleak, poetic, and human.  
A meditation on loss, persistence, and the strange beauty of broken worlds.
